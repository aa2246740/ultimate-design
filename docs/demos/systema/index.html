03 — Venue

Meridian Hall

A single auditorium with clear sightlines, working Wi-Fi, and room enough for hallway debates between talks.

Address

Meridian Hall
400 Grid Avenue
Meridian Hall District

Travel

Arrive via Line B to Grid Station (6-minute walk) or park in Lot M under the hall. Doors open at 09:00; badge pickup closes at 09:25. Bring a laptop if you want to sketch during workshops — power strips sit on every other row.

Access

Step-free entry on the west side. Hearing loop available in the main hall. Request assistance at register@systema.example before 9 October.

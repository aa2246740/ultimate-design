Full INCI list ships with every bottle. Batch cards list distill dates for the vetiver and cedar.

Apply two sprays to pulse points after dressing. Avoid rubbing; let the top notes settle for three minutes before layering fabric.

No.04 holds closest to skin in cool air and opens wider in heated rooms. One application lasts through an evening; a third spray on a scarf extends the drydown without crowding the room.

Pairs cleanly with unscented moisturizer. Skip heavy vanilla or musk bases on the same day so the vetiver can remain legible.
